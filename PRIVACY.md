# Privacy Policy

**Shorts Blocker collects no data.**

- It has **no INTERNET permission**, so it cannot send anything off your device. You can verify this in `app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`.
- It uses an Android Accessibility Service restricted to two apps: YouTube (`com.google.android.youtube`) and Instagram (`com.instagram.android`). It only checks whether the Shorts or Reels screen is showing and, if so, presses Back.
- It does **not** read, record, log, or store what you watch, search, type, message, or any personal information.
- The only data stored is your on/off switches and counters of how many Shorts/Reels were blocked. They live in the app's private storage on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- The notification permission is used only to show the "Shorts Blocker is ON" status notification and the optional reminder pop-ups.
- It contains no ads, analytics, trackers, or third-party libraries.

Questions or concerns? Open an issue on GitHub.
